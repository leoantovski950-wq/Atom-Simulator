# Physics documentation

## Hydrogenic energies

\[
E_n = -\frac{Z^2 R_\infty hc}{n^2}
\]

where \(R_\infty\) is the Rydberg constant (infinite nuclear mass).

Converted to eV via \(E/\mathrm{e}\).

Reference values (Z=1):

| n | E (eV, precise) | Textbook |
|---|-----------------|----------|
| 1 | −13.605693123   | −13.6    |
| 2 | −3.401423281    | −3.4     |

## Transition 2 → 1

Photon energy = \(E_2 - E_1\) (emission) ≈ 10.20427 eV  
Wavelength (vacuum) ≈ 121.502 nm  

Textbook value 121.6 nm lies within the 0.2 nm tolerance used in tests. Difference arises from infinite-mass approximation and rounding.

## Coulomb force

\[
F = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}
\]

Validated at \(q_1=q_2=e\), \(r=1\,\text{Å}\).

## Explicit non-claims

- No reduced-mass correction.
- No fine structure / Lamb shift.
- No multi-electron quantum treatment.
- No time-dependent wave-function propagation.
- Bohr orbits are classical comparison quantities only.
